Raisin

One login. Dozens of top-yield FDIC-insured banks.

Raisin is a marketplace that spreads your savings across a network of community banks and credit unions, so you can chase best-in-class APYs without opening (or moving) individual accounts. Each partner bank carries its own $250K FDIC insurance — deposits are held at the banks, not at Raisin.

Why we like it: no monthly fees, no minimum balance requirements, and rate transparency you don't get from a big-bank savings account.

Fidelity HSA

The only major HSA that lets you invest every dollar from day one.

Most HSA providers make you keep $1,000–$2,000 in cash as a "spend floor" before you can invest — money that quietly loses purchasing power to inflation. Fidelity's HSA doesn't. Every contribution can go straight into low-cost index funds if that's what you want.

Why we like it: same fund lineup and pricing as a Fidelity brokerage, triple tax advantage (pre-tax in, tax-free growth, tax-free out for qualified medical), and the balance rolls forward forever — no use-it-or-lose-it.
